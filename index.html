About KSMT

East Java's Agricultural Processing Specialist

PT Karya Setya Mustikatama (KSMT) is an agricultural processing company based in Lumajang, East Java — Indonesia's productive agricultural region known for its paddy, rice and horticultural output.

We specialise in the milling and processing of grain (gabah), rice (beras) and onion (bawang) — supplying distributors, food manufacturers, wholesalers and institutional buyers with consistently-processed agricultural commodities.

Our facility sits at Jl. Raya Lumajang–Tempeh KM 7, at the centre of East Java's agricultural production corridor, giving us direct access to quality raw material from local farmers and producers.

How We Work

From Farm to Your Supply Chain

Five controlled steps from raw harvest to finished, market-ready product.

01
🌾

Receiving

Raw paddy, grain and onion received from local East Java farmers and suppliers. Graded for quality and moisture on arrival.

02
☀️

Drying

Paddy and grain dried to optimal moisture content for milling. Consistent drying ensures longer shelf life and better milling yield.

03
⚙️

Milling

Hulling, whitening and polishing — paddy is converted to clean white rice (beras) with consistent grain size and appearance.

04
✅

Grading & QC

Finished product is graded for size, colour and cleanliness. Broken grain is separated. Onion is sized and sorted for market standards.

05
🚚

Packaging & Dispatch

Packed per buyer specification — sacks, bulk or retail packaging. Dispatched reliably to distributors and buyers across Indonesia.

FAQ

Common Questions

What is the minimum order quantity?+
Minimum order is 1 ton for rice and grain, and 500 kg for onion. We also accommodate regular standing supply agreements — weekly, fortnightly or monthly — based on your distribution needs.
What packaging is available?+
Standard packaging is 25 kg and 50 kg woven sacks. Bulk delivery (per truck, unpackaged) is available for buyers with own storage. Custom packaging or labelling can be discussed for regular buyers.
Is supply available year-round?+
Rice (beras) is available year-round from our milled stock. Grain (gabah) supply is highest during and after harvest season (typically May–August and November–January in East Java). Onion peaks May–August when Lumajang's harvest is at its largest. We will advise on availability when you enquire.
What area do you deliver to?+
We supply buyers across East Java with our own vehicles, and can arrange trucking to other Java provinces. For buyers outside Java, we can coordinate with your freight forwarder for inter-island shipment from Surabaya.
Can we arrange a regular supply agreement?+
Yes — we work with regular buyers on standing supply agreements with agreed volume, frequency and pricing. Contact us to discuss your ongoing requirements and we will propose appropriate terms.

About KSMT

East Java's Agricultural Processing Specialist

PT Karya Setya Mustikatama (KSMT) is an agricultural processing company based in Lumajang, East Java — one of Indonesia's most productive agricultural regions. We specialise in the milling and processing of grain (gabah), rice (beras) and onion (bawang) for B2B buyers.

Our facility is located at Jl. Raya Lumajang–Tempeh KM 7, a strategic position at the heart of East Java's grain and rice producing corridor. We supply distributors, food industry companies, wholesalers and institutional buyers with consistent, quality-processed agricultural products.

As part of the Mustikatama Group, we apply the same standards of operational excellence and business integrity that define all of the Group's industrial operations across Lumajang.

What We Process

Grain, Rice & Onion Processing

We process three core agricultural commodities for B2B buyers — distributors, food manufacturers and wholesalers — across Indonesia.

Primary Commodity

Grain (Gabah)

Paddy grain (gabah) milling and processing. We receive, clean, dry and mill raw paddy into cleaned grain ready for further processing or distribution to rice mills and food manufacturers.

Applications

  • Rice mill supply
  • Food processing industry
  • Grain trading & distribution
  • Institutional buyers
Core Product

Rice (Beras)

Rice milling from paddy to finished white rice (beras). Processed to consistent quality, grain size and moisture content for wholesalers, distributors and food service buyers across East Java and beyond.

Applications

  • Wholesale distribution
  • Modern retail supply
  • Institutional catering
  • Export preparation
Speciality

Onion (Bawang)

Onion cleaning, grading and processing. Lumajang is one of Indonesia's primary onion producing regions — KSMT provides B2B processing services for local producers and traders requiring consistent, market-ready supply.

Applications

  • Horticultural trading
  • Food industry supply
  • Supermarket distribution
  • Export grading

About KSMT

East Java's Agricultural Processing Partner

PT Karya Setya Mustikatama (KSMT) is an agricultural commodity processing company based in Lumajang, East Java — one of Indonesia's most productive rice and horticultural regions.

We source paddy directly from local East Java farmers, mill and process it at our facility, and supply white rice, paddy grain and red shallot to distributors, food manufacturers, wholesalers and institutional buyers across Indonesia.

Our facility sits at Jl. Raya Lumajang–Tempeh KM 7, at the heart of East Java's agricultural corridor — giving us direct, cost-effective access to raw material and the ability to maintain consistent supply year-round.

How We Work

From Field to Delivery

Five controlled steps — from raw harvest to finished, market-ready product.

👨‍🌾
Step 1
Farmer sourcing / paddy field harvest
01
Paddy Sourcing

Raw paddy, grain and onion received direct from local East Java farmers. Graded for quality and moisture at intake.

☀️
Step 2
Paddy drying — sun drying yard or mechanical dryer
02
Drying

Paddy dried to ≤14% moisture for optimal milling yield and longer shelf life. Consistent drying reduces broken grain.

⚙️
Step 3
Rice milling machines — hulling, whitening, polishing equipment
03
Milling

Hulling, whitening and polishing — paddy converted to clean white rice with consistent grain size and appearance.

📦
Step 4
Packaging line — sacks being filled, sealed and labelled for delivery
04
Grading & Packaging

Finished rice graded by size and cleanliness. Packed into 5kg–50kg bags or bulk as specified by buyer.

Our Story

Rooted in East Java's
Agricultural Tradition

PT Karya Setya Mustikatama (KSMT) is an agricultural commodity processing company based in Lumajang, East Java — one of Indonesia's most productive rice and horticultural regions.

For over three decades, we have worked alongside local farming communities to source, mill and process paddy, white rice and red shallot — supplying distributors, food manufacturers, wholesalers and institutional buyers across Indonesia.

Our facility at Jl. Raya Lumajang–Tempeh KM 7 sits at the heart of East Java's agricultural corridor, giving us direct access to the finest local produce and the capacity to maintain consistent year-round supply.

How We Work

From Field to Delivery

Four controlled steps — from raw harvest to finished, market-ready product.

👨‍🌾
Step 1
Farmer sourcing or paddy field
Step 01
Paddy Sourcing

Raw paddy and produce received direct from local East Java farmers. Graded on arrival for quality and moisture.

☀️
Step 2
Sun-drying yard or mechanical dryer
Step 02
Drying

Paddy dried to ≤14% moisture — optimal for milling yield and shelf life. Consistent drying reduces broken grain.

⚙️
Step 3
Milling equipment — hulling, whitening, polishing
Step 03
Milling

Hulling, whitening and polishing converts paddy to clean white rice with consistent grain size and appearance.

📦
Step 4
Packaging line — sacks filled and sealed
Step 04
Grading & Packaging

Finished product graded for size and cleanliness. Packed in 5 kg–50 kg bags or bulk as specified.
